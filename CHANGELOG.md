## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-01

### Added
- Initial release of StayNest MERN full-stack homestay booking platform.
- Authentication system with guest and host roles (JWT, bcrypt).
- Listing management endpoints and host dashboard.
- Search and filtering by city, stay type, guests, and price.
- Date-range booking system with automatic night and price calculations.
- My Trips page with booking cancellation support.
- Review and rating system for completed stays.
